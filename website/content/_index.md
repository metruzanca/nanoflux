{{< lead >}}
A dead-simple, self-hosted RSS reader. Go backend, htmx UI, and SQLite — own your feeds, your way.
{{< /lead >}}

## Why nanoflux

- **Automagical adds** — drop in a URL and nanoflux finds the feed, the title, and the author.
- **Profiles** — one author, many feeds; feeds can be authorless and assigned later.
- **Collections** — group feeds into folders.
- **Self-hosted** — one static binary, one SQLite file, easy Docker deployment.
- **Multi-user** — username/password accounts per instance.

## Get started

The project is free and open source. Grab the source on
[GitHub](https://github.com/metruzanca/nanoflux), run it with Docker or podman,
and you're reading within minutes.
